## 2. Reconciliation (step 1)

- **HEAD** was `02645a4` with a clean tree at the start.
- **Reproduction is exact.** Nine committed v9/v10 tables were re-generated by executing the committed notebook cells. The maximum difference is 4e-16 (`reproduction_check.csv`).
- **The incremental-IC discrepancy is two different tests on two different samples.**
  - v9's +0.005 is *FCI → +EPS*. v10's +0.058 is *EPS → +FCI*.
  - Both reproduce exactly on their own months.
  - Identity: ΔIC(FCI→+EPS) − ΔIC(EPS→+FCI) = IC_EPS − IC_FCI, which holds to 1e-16. So the two directions are one fact, not two pieces of evidence.
  - Same-observation results are in `incremental_reconciliation.csv`, and the month lists are in `incremental_observation_sets.csv`.

  | Sample (validation, 3M) | n | FCI→+EPS ΔIC (t) | EPS→+FCI ΔIC (t) |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | v9 holed | 92 | +0.005 (0.10) | +0.024 (0.40) |
  | v10 holed | 89 | −0.015 (−0.30) | +0.058 (1.08) |
  | **CLEAN** | 103 | +0.029 (0.57) | +0.025 (0.46) |

  **Neither direction adds significantly in validation on any sample.** Incremental value is not established.

- **Why the v9 and v10 samples differ: three causes**
  1. Start date. v9 starts 2009-12. v10 starts 2010-11, because 60M z-scores of P/E need history.
  2. **Holes.** Gated signals (`x.where(cond, 0)`) become constant across all five markets whenever the condition fails everywhere. The cross-sectional z-score is then 0/0, and the month was dropped *for every signal*.
     - v9 dropped 16 months (2016-03..07, 2020-03..2021-01), caused by TECH_t3.
     - v10 dropped 27 months (2011-09..12, 2015-08..2016-06, 2018-06..2019-03, 2020-03..09), caused by two gated valuation signals.
     - The dropped months are stress months: VIX averaged 23.5 in them vs 17.4 in the kept months.
     - In those months FCI's IC was −0.026 and EPS's was +0.330. Censoring flattered FCI and penalised EPS.
  3. **CLEAN sample used from v11 on.** It contains the months where FCI and EPS are both defined: 2009-05..2026-04, 204 months, no holes.
     - Discovery runs to 2016-12; embargo 2017-01..06; validation 2017-07..2026-01 for 3M (103 IC months) and to 2025-10 for 6M.
- **Semideviation fix is verified.** It matches an independent loop to 6e-17 and has full coverage (233 months per market from 2006-12). The pre-fix construction had zero valid months.

**Macro FCI 6M**
- The **US** is the largest contributor at 3M. Without it, the 3M validation IC is not distinguishable from zero.
- At 6M the result holds ex-US and survives drift removal. **6M is the more defensible horizon.**
- **Japan contributes negatively** in every window.
- Switzerland's FCI is the euro-area GSEAFCI, so there are effectively four independent FCI readings.
- 3M is concentrated: the top 10% of months make up 86% of the IC sum. 6M is broader at 48%.
- Specification sensitivity:
  - Positive under every variant tested: 3/9/12M lookback, rolling vs expanding scaling, z-score, unscaled, local currency, 1M lag.
  - Rolling-36M scaling is the weakest variant (val t 1.00 at 6M).
- **Correction to earlier write-ups:** the "36" in `own_sd(…, 36)` is a minimum-period burn-in of an *expanding* sd, not a 36M window. The 24, 36 and 60 settings are identical once defined.

**Micro EPS revision 6M**
- The time-average signal ranks the **US (+0.48) and Japan (+0.18–0.39) high**.
- The US outperformed by about 5% a year over the sample. A constant ranking would have scored a validation IC of +0.18. **Most of the EPS "edge" is that static tilt, not timing.**
- The **UK** is the largest timing contributor: time-series t 2.25 in validation.
- Reporting lag:
  - At 3M, a 1M lag barely matters (IC +0.132 vs +0.131).
  - A 2–3M lag reduces validation IC to +0.092 / +0.058.
  - At 6M, any lag removes it.
- US `Monthly_EPS` declines in only 20% of months, versus 31–42% elsewhere. It is smoother, which feeds the static US tilt.
- No fiscal-year roll artefact. Calendar-seasonality F-tests are insignificant for 4 of 5 markets, and the pattern is common to all markets.

**Not tested and not adopted:** the own-sd-scaled EPS variant looked better (val t 2.63). Adopting it would be selection on the validation window, so it stays a note.
